# Privacy Policy - Genichiro Translator

Genichiro Translator translates text between Arabic and English.

**What the extension does with your data**
- Text you type or paste, and text recognized from screenshots you paste, is sent to the Google Translate web service (translate.googleapis.com) only to obtain the translation. Google's own privacy policy applies to that request.
- Screenshots are read on your device (OCR runs locally inside the extension). The images themselves are never uploaded or stored.
- The clipboard is read only when you click PASTE or press Ctrl+V in the popup, and written only when you click COPY.

**What we do NOT do**
- We do not collect, store, sell or share your data.
- We do not use analytics, ads or trackers.
- We do not run any server; the developer never receives your text or images.
